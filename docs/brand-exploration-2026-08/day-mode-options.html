Six grounds for daylight reading.

The shipped ground is already 96% light and body ink scores 14.9 against it, so this is not a contrast problem. What is missing is separation: cards sit 1.06 from the ground, which is invisible, so every surface collapses into one field and the whole screen reads flat and grey. These six vary two things independently — how light the paper is, and how far cards lift off it — so you can tell which one you are actually reacting to.
