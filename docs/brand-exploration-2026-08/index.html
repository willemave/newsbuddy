Newsbuddy,
a made thing.

Logo & app-icon directions for the Newsly → Newsbuddy rebrand, in eight rounds. One read the Japanese brief literally. Two cut everything down to a flat glyph — too far. Three found the tactile, illustrated register, but made every idea an origami variation and every palette orange. Four kept the craft and the calm, dropped the folding, and gave each concept its own color. Five took the three survivors and put each through eight palettes. Six went darker still and crossed the survivors with each other. Seven put the last two through twelve palettes apiece. Eight recolors them by editing the originals rather than re-describing them, and reduces both to two flat inks. Every option carries an extracted color kit and a mock of the real app — the continuous editorial Briefing column and the long-form reader — rendered in that kit.
