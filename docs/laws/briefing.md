# Briefing Laws

B1. Briefing is a per-user, continuously updated reading edition.

Normal refreshes for the same user never perform paid work concurrently; different
users may progress independently. Pending Full rebuilds retain their intent, and
sources arriving during a refresh remain durable for its successor. Readiness wakes
cannot erase provider backoff or bypass current source/filter authorization.
Incremental bounded routing leaves undispatched sources pending rather than assigning or
silently dropping them. A Full rebuild keeps undecided sources in general news and
never retires eligible published coverage absent from its replacement.

Briefing and the Knowledge list share the same bottom-edge fade: short, faint, and confined to the bottom screen edge, so content stays readable right up to and through the floating navigation. Floating navigation and the pinned Briefing pills and player are translucent glass that content visibly scrolls beneath; Briefing content runs up under the status bar without a top fade. Floating root navigation keeps the selected destination as a compact highlighted icon and expands each unselected destination to show its name.

B2. Eligible sources are completed, unread articles, podcasts, and canonical ready news that the authenticated user can open directly. Legacy editorial `to_read`/`skip` classification has no eligibility authority; lifecycle failures, skipped processing, archive state, and readiness remain distinct.

B3. A source key is the canonical unit of coverage, citation, and read state. Briefing passage links retain their accent color and tap destination without an underline.

B4. Article and podcast segments cover one source each, while news segments may combine several sources into a compact roundup. News prose follows a concise newspaper-brief style: a single-event passage never exceeds 40 words, while a multi-event roundup targets 45-65 words and never exceeds 75. Linked descriptive noun phrases appear early and fit grammatically into their sentence without duplicating a predicate. Supporting prose selects useful context or material qualifications without repeating the link, substituting a feature inventory, or expanding merely to reach a target; thematic connections appear only when informative. A news segment is sized by the distinct events it covers, not by source count: sources covering one event always stay in the same segment, however many there are.

B5. Lens names and order remain stable within one representation, and counts describe active unread source coverage.

Articles and Podcasts display each work's Briefing date and exact local time in its headline metadata alongside the publication and reading time or duration. These lists have no separate date/time dividers; News retains its timeline dividers. Metadata remains readable at narrow widths and larger text sizes.

B6. Every admitted source remains reachable exactly once while eligible. Duplicate reconciliation, refresh, and compaction preserve that coverage.

B7. One user-scoped version identifies the complete visible Briefing. Every visible mutation changes it, and an unchanged private validator may return `304 Not Modified`.

B8. Paging at one fixed version returns the same ordered segments, sources, read flags, and summary as the complete lens.

B9. Refreshes may coalesce, but publication requires successful composition and unchanged version, source ownership, and eligibility. A manual refresh observes its exact durable task, so successful same-version completion terminates without waiting for a version-change deadline. Failed or stale work leaves the last usable edition intact.

B10. Server state remains authoritative. Local snapshots support cold starts, and recoverable failures, retries, or reopening preserve readable content while unfinished work resumes safely.

B11. A segment becomes read only after it was visible and its full rendered body passes above the readable viewport boundary. Initial offscreen geometry never marks it read.

B12. Reading a segment marks its full source batch once, and the segment retires when every source is read. The current reading view may retain that segment briefly for positional stability, but an explicit list refresh removes server-retired segments without recomposing the edition. Marking a lens read covers every active source and canonical duplicate representative. A successful read-mutation response is durable and visible to the next Briefing index read.

B13. Read-only styling preserves scroll position and cannot be reversed by an index response from before an accepted read mutation. Outside that reconciliation window, the latest server index remains authoritative even when its version is lower. Replacing the ordered document resets the lens to the top without interrupting the readable view during refresh.

B14. Links, figures, discussions, and citations resolve to sources owned by their segment, while invalid references are repaired or rejected before publication. Citation validation, coverage, rendering, and narration agree on actual Markdown links, including nested or escaped brackets and Unicode titles. Bare URI text, code, and images do not establish citation coverage. Article and podcast passages treat each source as a full work, use its available enriched context, and give its thesis, evidence or counterpoints, and significance substantive treatment. Each article or podcast segment opens with a headline that links to its source: the publication or show name without feed taglines, the work's title, and its kind with reading time or episode length when known. The passage prose does not restate that title or publication; it opens with the work's central idea and links the source on a descriptive phrase. News roundups carry no headline. Pullquotes are editorial callouts and never claim to be source quotations, while Dig Deeper uses the selected passage with user-visible support.

B15. First-run progress is durable and incremental. One unavailable source cannot block later sources or remove categories already ready to read. Healthy warm news attaches without an onboarding-specific aggregator fetch or repeated matching story embeddings, including event grouping. A newly demanded Brutalist topic can trigger a shared fetch; its first-run source cannot settle against an older check while that fetch is active. A source with no qualifying successful check becomes unavailable after a bounded wait. Initial incremental refreshes review at most one bounded parallel batch of category proposals and publish one complete news segment at a time. Mixed or individually failed proposals cannot hold successful sibling categories behind serial publication work; failed proposals remain pending, and every accepted category still meets the same coherence and minimum-support rules. New categories take precedence over additional segments in existing categories; a cooling input cannot block an eligible sibling. A productive first-run publication advances the next refresh promptly even when some embeddings are still pending; an unproductive refresh retains bounded retry backoff. Full rebuilds preserve complete source coverage. The first news cohort is bounded before admission, remains stable across retries, and settles before remaining eligible news enters normal admission; deferred news is never silently marked read. Normal follow-up admission, semantic assignment, and news composition preparation proceed in bounded batches; an accumulated backlog cannot turn one refresh into unbounded matching work, and older eligible stories remain available for later batches.

Enabled Articles and Podcasts lenses are available immediately, including while their feeds are still processing. During the first run, Welcome and the available lenses share one navigation row; opening a news category does not duplicate its pill, and readable categories retain their mark-all-read action. Progress follows the run's admitted items, including reused content, and distinguishes discovery, preparation, readable publication, failure, and skipped items. A summary awaiting lens publication is still preparing. Empty tiers offer source management. Browsing a lens preserves ongoing progress, and refresh validators cannot hide progress changes. Terminal artwork failures and ineligible or archived items cannot keep a tier preparing forever. An abandoned first run expires after 24 hours without affecting its subscriptions, content, or normal processing. A completed aggregator check belongs to that run and does not regress when later polling discovers more news. Ready article and podcast publication never waits for missing news lens embeddings, and one failed story does not change healthy siblings' semantic assignment.

B16. Briefing audio is a grounded listening adaptation of the lens selected when Play is requested. Its complete eligible unread source set defines the program boundary, and playback never automatically continues into another lens. Article and podcast programs keep one titled source per chapter. News chapters may curate only the highest-signal details within that lens; naturally finishing a News chapter marks its complete planned source window read even when some sources were not spoken.

B17. News categories can adapt overnight in the user's reported timezone. Maintenance uses recent eligible stories and existing matching embeddings; recent read stories can inform category meaning without becoming unread again. Unknown timezones do not imply UTC. Existing composed segments, citations, read identities, and narration stay intact when routing changes; a category that stops accepting news remains readable until its pending and composed coverage drains.

Fitted, unpublished stories are re-decided against newly saved category definitions by the normal refresh. Stories outside the maintenance fitting set retain their existing drain behavior.

When a category is first named, its title and description must fit every accepted member. Grouping stories for review cannot itself establish that they share a theme. A story excluded from the proposed theme remains reachable through general news; exclusion never marks it read or removes it from the first-run cohort. The minimum support for a named category remains unchanged.

Category definitions and assignments belong to the individual user and remain durable across refreshes. Assignment to an existing category requires a confident decision based on the story's summary evidence and that user's current category definitions. Membership follows what the story mainly reports; supporting tools, vendors, contracts, or funding do not establish membership unless they are themselves the main news. Explicit category restrictions and exclusions remain authoritative. Similarity alone never establishes membership, including at the category limit. Uncertain or unavailable decisions remain accessible through general news. A confident unmatched story can inform new-category discovery while capacity remains. Sources outside a newly named category's reviewed sample wait for a decision against its saved definition during incremental refreshes; full rebuilds retain their coverage in general news. They are not automatically admitted by proximity. Category or source changes during inference invalidate stale assignments.

Semantic identity survives only when overlap and meaning support continuity. New recurring topics need at least three distinct events and support on two consecutive local nights before publication. At the category limit, unsupported old topics cannot block discovery of supported new topics, and published categories cannot erase a candidate's accumulated nightly support. Weak matches remain reachable in a separate general-news category. Category naming reviews up to 25 representative stories per category in one bounded batch of at most 10 semantic categories; unchanged names are valid outcomes. Shadow maintenance records proposals without changing the reading edition, and candidate identity can persist independently of published routing.

B18. Long-pressing a source link in News, Articles, or Podcasts Briefing offers Add to Knowledge, Chat, and Create Deck, including source headlines and linked figures or quotes. Saving is idempotent; a news source saves its linked article, while articles and podcasts save the source itself. Chat resumes the source's latest chat or opens a new source composer without sending a message. Create Deck opens the source's existing deck or generation status when available, otherwise offers the optional-focus creation form. Successful actions refresh Knowledge. Opening the menu alone leaves read state unchanged, normal taps still open the source, and text selection retains Dig Deeper.

B19. A person may apply one plain-language preference to upcoming News in Briefing. Articles and Podcasts bypass it. Supported onboarding preserves the person's original typed or spoken interests, shows an editable seed and opt-out, and initializes it once; existing accounts and clients omitting the seed remain off. Settings exposes a dedicated News Filter row near the top. Explicit edits, clearing, and disabling survive later onboarding and concurrent updates cannot silently overwrite them.

B20. News is excluded only by an applicable confident negative decision. Uncertain or unusable evidence and unavailable inference remain eligible; failure cannot override a known exclusion. Unevaluated, outside-window, and budget-deferred sources remain accessible in All News and cannot be described as model exclusions. Exclusions do not consume the admission quota. All News and Filtered Out retain normal source authorization and typed News actions.

B21. Preference edits affect unpublished News only. Changes during inference or composition cannot publish a stale exclusion or admission; summary changes invalidate decisions. Published coverage remains readable through refresh, compaction, and full rebuild. Filtered or deferred first-run sources settle without being marked read or reported as processing failures. Preview never applies a preference or changes durable admission decisions.
